## {{KEY PRINCIPLE 1}}

{{2-3 PARAGRAPHS ON THIS PRINCIPLE, INCLUDING WHAT IT EXCLUDES}}

*Typical principle categories to consider:*
- **Privacy posture** — where does user data live, who can access it, what's reversible?
- **Automation ethics** — when does the system act autonomously vs. propose-then-confirm?
- **Data integrity** — what does "correct" mean for your domain, and what's non-negotiable?
- **Monetization stance** — how does this product make money, and what does it refuse to do?
- **Open vs. closed** — are data formats portable, are integrations extensible, can users self-host?

Each principle should be 2–3 paragraphs. Explain what it is, why it's non-negotiable, and what it rules out. Be specific — vague principles don't constrain decisions.
